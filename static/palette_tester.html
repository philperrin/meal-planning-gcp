Meal Planning Assistant

👤 demo.user@example.com

Welcome!

I'm your intelligent meal planning assistant - designed to streamline your weekly dinner planning. I'll help generate balanced meal plans, schedule complete meals onto your Google Calendar, and create a consolidated grocery list.
This app was developed by Phil at Mile High Data Viz.

Step 1 ⚙️

Getting Started

Go to the Settings page to confirm you have an active API key connected.

Step 2 ❤️

Setting Preferences

Set your Preferences for allergies, preferred cuisines, diner count, and meal times.

Step 3 🗓️

Generate Meal Plans

Use the Planner tab to generate customized dinners - creating a shopping list and calendar events for each meal.

Step 4 📜

Review Past Meals

Use the History tab to review previous recipes, add recipes to a 'Favorites' list, and re-use recipes.

Input specific ideas or needs for certain meals this week (e.g. 'I'd like one pizza night that includes margherita pizza')
Select one or more presets. One meal in the meal plan will include the presets (not all meals will have all presets applied).

🔑 Configuration & API Keys

• Active Key Connected
Required to run the Gemini AI recipe generation models. The app includes a shared starter key, or you can supply your own personal key from Google AI Studio for dedicated rate limits.

App Deployment & Information

This system runs entirely serverless within your Google Account. All recipe details and meal history are saved securely in your Google Drive inside the file Automated_Meal_Planner_DB.json.

Upon meal plan approval, the assistant will automatically generate:

  • Prep & cook events scheduled on your default Google Calendar with complete recipe details, scaled ingredients, and cooking instructions.
  • A dedicated 🛒 Groceries checklist event on your Google Calendar containing your complete grocery list.

Data Privacy & How Your Information Is Handled

All your personal data—including dietary preferences, allergies, custom recipes, meal history, and approved meal plans—is stored exclusively in your private Google Drive in the Automated_Meal_Planner_DB.json file. No third-party ad tracking or marketing analytics are used.
